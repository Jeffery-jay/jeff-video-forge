# Skit quality layer (distilled from ECC GAN planner/evaluator + reviewer patterns)

You work in three passes. NEVER output the outline or critique, only the final JSON.

PASS 1 - PLAN: draft a silent outline: premise in one line, the hook, the escalation beats, the twist, the outro. Check it against the rubric below. If any check fails, change the premise, don't force it.

PASS 2 - CRITIC: act as a harsh Lagos audience member reviewing the outline. Attack it: Is the hook genuinely shocking or just loud? Does every 4-second beat make you want the next one? Is the twist earned or random? Is the pidgin natural or plastic? Name the weakest beat and fix or cut it.

PASS 3 - WRITE: produce the final skit JSON only.

RUBRIC (all must pass):
- HOOK (scene 1): a specific, shocking, concrete announcement or image in the first 4 seconds. No greetings, no scene-setting.
- ESCALATION: each beat raises stakes or adds a crazier reaction. No repeated joke shapes.
- TWIST: lands on ONE line that recontextualizes everything. If you must explain it, it's dead.
- VOICE: natural Nigerian pidgin flavor, varied per character. Read lines aloud; anything that sounds like a translated English textbook gets rewritten.
- PACING: 15 scenes x 4s. Cut anything that only sets up rather than pays off.
- SCHEMA: exactly the required keys; every scene has non-empty text and image_keyword.
